Getting there

The last mile

  1. Take Route 44 west to Barkhamsted.
  2. Turn right onto Route 318 at the light, signed Pleasant Valley.
  3. Cross the green steel bridge.
  4. Turn left immediately onto East River Road.
  5. Drive about a mile north. The entrance is on the left.

Coming from

  • West Hartford25 miles, 40 minutes. Route 44 west to the Route 318 light.
  • Hartford and I-9130 miles, 45 minutes. Route 44 west to Barkhamsted.
  • Waterbury, New Haven, and New YorkRoute 8 north to Winsted, Route 44 east a few miles, left at the Route 318 light. From New York City, about 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • Boston and the Mass PikeI-84 west to Hartford, Route 44 west to Barkhamsted. About 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Springfield and points northI-91 south to Route 20 west, through Granby to Riverton, then East River Road south about 4 miles. About 1 hour.
  • Bradley Airport (BDL)30 miles, 50 minutes. Route 20 west to Riverton, then East River Road south.

Parking
