Where to stay

Beds between West Hartford and Riverton, sorted by how far they are from the forest. Every inn and hotel has a phone number you can tap. Drive times are for a normal Saturday morning.

Book early

Saturday, October 10 is the start of Columbus Day weekend, and the second weekend of October is usually the peak of the foliage season in the Litchfield Hills. The small inns nearest the forest have only a handful of rooms and fill first. If you plan to stay over, please call now.

Every place here was checked in September 2026. Prices change with the season, so we have not listed them.

A bite nearby

The village of Riverton is a few minutes up the river road from the forest, and New Hartford is a few minutes back toward Route 44.

  • RivertonThe Royal Coachman Tavern at the Old Riverton Inn, and the Riverton General Store for coffee and sandwiches.
  • New HartfordThe New Hartford Diner on Route 44 for breakfast before you head up.
  • GroceriesThe last full-size grocery stores on the way are in Canton and Winsted on Route 44.
