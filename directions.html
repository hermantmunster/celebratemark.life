Getting there

Peoples State Forest picnic shelter, East River Road, Barkhamsted, Connecticut 06063. Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 11:00 AM.

Before you leave the house

Cell service comes and goes in the river valley, so map apps can lose their way near the end. Read the last mile below once before you set out, or send this page to your phone.

41.92333, -72.99833

The last mile

Everyone arrives the same way, whichever direction you come from. This is the part to remember.

  1. Get to Route 44 in Barkhamsted. Route 44 is the main road that runs from Hartford west through Avon, Canton, and New Hartford toward Winsted.
  2. Turn onto Route 318 at the traffic light in Barkhamsted, signed for Pleasant Valley. Coming from the Hartford side, Route 318 is a right turn. Coming from Winsted, it is a left.
  3. Cross the steel bridge over the Farmington River. It is a short, green truss bridge; you will know it when you see it.
  4. Turn left immediately after the bridge onto East River Road. The turn comes fast, right at the end of the bridge.
  5. Drive about one mile north along the river. The Peoples State Forest recreation area entrance is on your left, between the road and the river. Park in the lot and walk the short path to the shelter, about 150 feet.

Coming from

Times are for a normal Saturday morning. Foliage traffic on Route 44 can add a few minutes, so leave a little early.

  • West HartfordAbout 25 miles, 40 minutes. Take Route 44 west the whole way, through Avon, Canton, and New Hartford, to the Route 318 light in Barkhamsted. Then follow the last mile above.
  • Hartford and I-91About 30 miles, 45 minutes. Get onto Route 44 west in West Hartford and follow it to Barkhamsted.
  • Waterbury, New Haven, and New YorkTake Route 8 north to Winsted. In Winsted pick up Route 44 east for a few miles to the Route 318 light in Barkhamsted, and turn left onto Route 318. From New York City, allow about 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • Boston and the Mass PikeI-84 west to Hartford, then Route 44 west through West Hartford to Barkhamsted. Allow about 2 hours 15 minutes from Boston.
  • Springfield and points northI-91 south to Route 20 west (or Route 44 west from Hartford). Route 20 runs west through Granby to Riverton; from Riverton, East River Road follows the river south about 4 miles to the recreation area. Allow about 1 hour from Springfield.
  • Bradley Airport (BDL)About 30 miles, 50 minutes. Route 20 west through Granby to Riverton, then East River Road south along the river, or Route 44 west from Hartford. Rental cars are at the terminal. There is no public transport to the forest.

At the park

  • ParkingFree for cars with Connecticut plates. Cars with out-of-state plates pay $15 on weekends at the entrance. The lot is about 150 feet from the shelter along a level park path.
  • If walking is hardThe park does not normally allow cars up to the pavilion, but it makes arrangements in advance for anyone who needs to be dropped closer. Please call the park office at (860) 379-2469 during the week before, or ask the family to arrange it for you on the RSVP form.
  • RestroomsFlush toilets and drinking water are at the picnic area, a short walk from the shelter.
  • The shelterA covered pavilion about 30 by 60 feet with picnic tables and a stone fireplace, open on the sides, beside the river. Seats about 100 under the roof, with room all around it.
  • HoursThe park opens at 8:00 AM. The shelter is ours until a half hour before sunset, about 5:45 PM.
  • DogsWelcome in the park on a leash.
  • Park office(860) 379-2469 · Peoples State Forest on ctparks.com
