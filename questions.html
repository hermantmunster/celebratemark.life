Questions

Tap a question. Not here? Ask in the note on the RSVP form.

When and where?

Saturday, October 10, 2026, 10 AM to 1 PM, at the pavilion at Peoples Forest, East River Road, Barkhamsted, Connecticut. Directions.

Is it outdoors? What if it rains?

Yes. The pavilion is large and covered, with open sides and a stone fireplace. Rain or shine. Bring a jacket; the valley is cool.

What should I wear?

Layers, a warm jacket, shoes for gravel and damp grass. Mark worked on job sites, not in ballrooms.

What about weather?

Mornings in the 40s, afternoons in the 50s or 60s, leaves near peak. Check a Barkhamsted forecast the week before.

Can I get closer if walking is hard?

The walk from parking is about 150 mostly level feet. The park will arrange a closer drop-off if asked ahead: call (860) 379-2469 the week before, or say so on the RSVP form. Seating and restrooms are close by.

Is there parking, and does it cost?

Yes, 150 feet from the pavilion. Connecticut plates free; out-of-state pay $15 on weekends. Bring a card and cash.

Are there restrooms?

Yes, with drinking water, at the picnic area a short walk away.

Will there be food?

The family is working out details and will update this page. Bring water. No cooking in the pavilion fireplace.

Is there a program?

The family will post the plan here once settled. To speak, or have something read for you, say so on the Share a memory page.

Can I bring children? A dog?

Both welcome. Dogs on a leash. Watch both near the water.

When does it end?

10 AM to 1 PM. Come when you can.

Flowers or donations?

Instead of flowers, a gift to Big Brothers Big Sisters of Connecticut. Mark was a dedicated Big Brother. A story about Mark is welcome too.

Will my phone work there?

Not reliably. Read the directions before you leave, or send them to your phone from that page.

I cannot be there. How can I take part?

Share a memory of Mark. With your permission the family may read it aloud.

Who do I contact?

The note on the RSVP form goes to Alex and Molly. For the park, call (860) 379-2469.

Mark Charles Gustafson, November 14, 1959 to August 28, 2026.
