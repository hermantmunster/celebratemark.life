Questions

Tap a question to open it. If yours is not here, the RSVP form has a place for notes, and the family will answer.

When and where, once more?

Saturday, October 10, 2026, beginning at 11:00 AM, at the pavilion at Peoples Forest, East River Road, Barkhamsted, Connecticut. Directions are here.

Is it outdoors? What if it rains?

Yes. The pavilion is large and covered, with open sides and a stone fireplace, beside the river. It has a roof, so we gather rain or shine. Bring a jacket either way; the river valley is cooler than the city.

What should I wear?

Whatever you would wear for a walk in the woods in October. Layers, a warm jacket, and shoes that can handle a gravel path and damp grass. There is no dress code. Mark spent his working life in job sites, not ballrooms.

What will the weather be like?

Early October in the Litchfield Hills usually means mornings in the 40s and afternoons in the 50s or 60s, with the leaves near their peak. Check a forecast for Barkhamsted the week before.

I have trouble walking. Can I get closer to the pavilion?

The walk from the parking lot to the pavilion is about 150 feet on a mostly level park path. The park does not normally allow cars up to the pavilion, but it does make arrangements in advance for anyone who needs a drop-off closer. Please call the park office at (860) 379-2469 the week before, or mention it in the note on the RSVP form and the family will arrange it. There will be seating at the pavilion, and the restrooms are close by.

Is there parking? Does it cost anything?

There is a parking lot about 150 feet from the pavilion. Cars with Connecticut plates park free. Cars with out-of-state plates pay $15 at the entrance on weekends. Bring a card or cash in case the attendant cannot take one or the other.

Are there restrooms?

Yes. Flush restrooms and drinking-water taps are at the picnic area, a short walk from the pavilion.

Will there be food?

The family is working out the details and will update this page. Either way, bring a water bottle; the day is long and the river air is dry. Please note the park does not allow the pavilion fireplace to be used for cooking.

Is there a program or a set time for speaking?

The family will share the shape of the day here once it is settled. If you would like to say a few words, or would like something read on your behalf, tell the family on the Share a memory page.

Can I bring children? A dog?

Children are welcome; it is a forest beside a river, with room to wander. Dogs are welcome in the park on a leash. Please keep an eye on both near the water.

When does it end?

Come when you can and stay as long as you like. The park asks everyone to leave the pavilion by a half hour before sunset, which is about 5:45 PM in October.

Flowers or donations?

Instead of flowers, the family asks you to consider a gift to Big Brothers Big Sisters of Connecticut. Mark was a dedicated Big Brother, and he loved the chance to guide anyone who needed it. A story about Mark is always welcome too.

Will my phone work there?

Not reliably. Cell service comes and goes in the river valley. Read the directions before you leave, or send them to your phone from that page, and tell anyone meeting you where and when.

I cannot be there. How can I take part?

Share a memory of Mark. The family will read every one, and with your permission may read some aloud on the day.

Who do I contact with a question?

Use the note on the RSVP form; it goes straight to Alex and Molly, and they read every message. For questions about the park itself, the park office is (860) 379-2469.

In memory of Mark Charles Gustafson, November 14, 1959 to August 28, 2026.
