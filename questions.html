Questions

When and where?

Saturday, October 10, 2026, 10 AM to 1 PM, at the pavilion at Peoples State Forest, East River Road, Barkhamsted, Connecticut. Directions.

Is it outdoors?

Yes, under a covered pavilion beside the river.

Will there be food?

The family is working out details and will update this page.

Is there a program?

The family will post the plan here once settled. To speak, or have something read for you, say so on the Share a memory page.

Can I bring children? A dog?

Yes, both. Dogs on a leash.

Flowers or donations?

Instead of flowers, a gift to Big Brothers Big Sisters of Connecticut. Mark was a Big Brother.

I cannot be there. How can I take part?

Share a memory of Mark. With your permission the family may read it aloud.

Who do I contact?

Alex and Molly. Write to them through the RSVP form. For the park office, call (860) 379-2469.

Mark Charles Gustafson, November 14, 1959 to August 28, 2026.
