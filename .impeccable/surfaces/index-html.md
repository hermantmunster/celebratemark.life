## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the trailhead kiosk you walk past on the day: a routed sign that says when and where before anything else. It refuses the cream page with a soft portrait and a candle.

OWN-WORLD: Routed brown pine board with cream letters; pine green and blaze orange as the only working colors; kiosk notice paper under the board for reading; a painted October ridge as the single ground picture; rounded router-cut letterforms in one sturdy sans (Rubik) with Atkinson Hyperlegible for prose; arrows and distances as the wayfinding grammar; every button is a routed plaque.

STORY: A visitor reads the date, time, and place in one glance, taps Directions or Add to calendar, then finds a bed, tells the family, and leaves a memory.
