Mark Charles Gustafson of West Hartford died on Friday, August 28, 2026. He was 66.

Mark built and ran Sonipoint, a company that designs and installs sound-masking systems, the quiet hum that lets people in busy offices hear themselves think. For years he did nearly all of it himself: the phone call, the drawings, the long drives, the day on a ladder, and the final tuning of every system by ear. His work is in hospitals, banks, universities, and offices from Connecticut to Texas to Toronto, and many of the people he worked with became friends for years.

He was a husband to Martha and a father to Alex, Molly, and Katie, and he is missed by all of them, by Kristyn, and by a wide circle of family, friends, neighbors, and colleagues.

The family will gather to celebrate his life on Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 11:00 AM at the picnic shelter in Peoples State Forest in Barkhamsted, on the Farmington River, a place they chose for its quiet, its tall pines, and the water. Everyone who knew Mark is welcome.

The family's full obituary will replace this note soon.
