The place

Peoples State Forest is in Pleasant Valley, Barkhamsted, on the east bank of the Farmington River. Fly fishing and the woods brought Mark peace; the pavilion sits in the pines beside the water.

Mark

Mark Charles Gustafson of West Hartford died August 28, 2026, at 66. Husband to Martha for 41 years, father of three, grandfather of four, owner of Sonipoint. He loved dogs, fly fishing, the woods, and a big party around his grill.
