The day, in short

Everything you need to know to get there and be comfortable. Full details are on the other pages.

  • WhenSaturday, October 10, 2026, beginning at 11:00 AM. Come when you can. The park asks everyone to leave the shelter by a half hour before sunset, about 5:45 PM.
  • WhereThe picnic shelter at Peoples State Forest, East River Road, Barkhamsted, CT 06063. The shelter is a covered pavilion with open sides, on the Farmington River. It is a short, level walk of about 150 feet from the parking area.
  • Getting thereFrom Route 44, take Route 318 in Barkhamsted, cross the steel bridge over the river, and turn left immediately onto East River Road. The recreation area is about a mile up on the left. Step-by-step directions and a map
  • ParkingFree for cars with Connecticut plates. Out-of-state plates pay $15 at the gate on weekends. If you cannot walk from the parking area, see the note about getting closer to the shelter.
  • What to wearWhatever is comfortable outdoors in October. Layers and sturdy shoes. Mornings in the river valley can be in the 40s; afternoons are usually in the 50s or 60s. The shelter has a roof, so we gather rain or shine.
  • RestroomsFlush restrooms and drinking water are at the picnic area, about 150 feet from the shelter.
  • Staying overOctober 10 is the Saturday of Columbus Day weekend and peak foliage in the Litchfield Hills. Rooms go early. Places to stay, from Riverton to West Hartford
  • FlowersInstead of flowers, the family asks you to consider a gift to Big Brothers Big Sisters of Connecticut. Mark was a dedicated Big Brother.

Getting there

Peoples State Forest is in the Pleasant Valley part of Barkhamsted, on the east bank of the Farmington River. Fly fishing and walks in the woods brought Mark peace, and the shelter sits in the pines beside the water. From West Hartford it is a straight run west on Route 44 through Avon, Canton, and New Hartford, then a few minutes on Route 318 and East River Road.

Cell service is unreliable in the river valley. Please look at the directions before you set out, or send them to your phone from the directions page.

Where to stay

There are beds within ten minutes of the forest in Riverton and New Hartford, more in Torrington and the Avon and Simsbury valley, and plenty in West Hartford and Farmington and near Bradley Airport. Every inn and hotel has an address and a phone number you can tap.

Please book early. It is a holiday weekend at the height of leaf season, and the small inns fill first.

Mark

Mark Charles Gustafson of West Hartford died on August 28, 2026, at 66. He was born in Ashtabula, Ohio, and met Martha, his wife of 41 years, at Ohio University. He ran his own sound-masking business, Sonipoint, across New England and the Tri-State area, and made friends wherever the long drives took him. He loved dogs, fly fishing, walks in the woods, and a big party around his grill, and he treasured his three children and four granddaughters.
